# Conceptual Modeling: Wave–Rotational Hypothesis

## Overview

This document provides conceptual models and diagrams to illustrate the wave–rotational hypothesis (WRH) for Earth–Moon dynamics. The models are designed to aid understanding of the theoretical framework without requiring deep mathematical expertise.

## 1. System Components

### 1.1 Classical Earth–Moon System

The classical model consists of:
- **Earth**: Rotating body with angular velocity ω_E
- **Moon**: Orbiting body with angular velocity ω_M
- **Tidal forces**: Energy dissipation mechanism
- **Angular momentum**: Conserved quantity exchanged between Earth rotation and Moon's orbit

### 1.2 Wave–Rotational Extension

The WRH adds:
- **Wave field**: Hypothetical field coupling rotation and orbital motion
- **Resonances**: Specific frequency relationships that enhance coupling
- **Phase alignment**: Relative orientation affecting interaction strength

The wave–rotational coupling can be understood by analogy to coupled pendulums:

```
Pendulum 1 (Earth rotation) ←→ [coupling spring] ←→ Pendulum 2 (Moon orbit)
```

Just as coupled pendulums exchange energy through a connecting spring, the Earth and Moon may exchange angular momentum through wave-mediated coupling.

## 5. Operational Definitions

### 5.1 Wave Field

**Conceptual definition**: A spatially-extended, time-varying influence that couples the rotational state of Earth to the orbital state of the Moon.

**Properties**:
- Periodic or quasi-periodic in time
- Possibly propagating or standing wave
- Very weak compared to gravitational and tidal forces
- Frequency in resonance with orbital/rotational periods

### 5.2 Coupling Mechanism

**Mechanism**: The wave field creates a torque that:
1. Depends on the relative phase of Earth's rotation and Moon's orbit
2. Acts over long time scales (centuries to millennia)
3. Produces small but cumulative effects
4. Is potentially detectable in precision measurements

### 5.3 Phase Alignment

**Phase angle** Δφ determines coupling strength:
- Δφ = 0°: Maximum constructive coupling
- Δφ = 90°: No net coupling
- Δφ = 180°: Maximum destructive coupling
